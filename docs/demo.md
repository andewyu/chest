# Demo script — literal lines

Fill this in on Friday evening. Write what you will actually type into the
phone, not a description of it.

## Beat 1 — the thread is the product (0:20–0:40)
> me: paid 47 for replacement hoses
> chest: Logged $47.00 expense — replacement hoses. Confirm?
> me: yes
> chest: Posted. Balance $9,720.

## Beat 2 — the buzz (0:40–1:20)
*Nothing typed. EventBridge fires the sweep.*
> chest: You're $14,849 short by Feb 1 — the summer program and the insurance
> renewal outran dues. I found a USDA opportunity that awards $10k–$25k and
> closes Nov 3. Draft's below.

## Beat 3 — provenance (1:20–2:10)
Point at a figure and its ledger id. Say the sentence:
"Every number in this application traces to a line in their books."

## Beat 4 — the approval, and the limit (2:10–2:30)
> me: YES
> chest: Approved and saved. You'll need to file it yourself at grants.gov —
> I don't submit applications.

Say on camera: **Chest never submits. A human files it.**
